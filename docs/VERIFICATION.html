LectureLivechecks for you

What LectureLive still needs from you

Everything the automated runs could not do: a real Zoom lecture, a pointer on the window, a prompt only macOS shows you, and two choices that are yours to make. Each item names the line of docs/milestones.md it comes from. Statuses and notes save in this browser; Export keeps a copy.

Last updated 26 September 2026, after M5 (main at 394bd13).

At your next Zoom lecture

Four checks, done in the order of a class. They close the three gate lines left open since M0 and M1, and answer the one question M5 could not: whether Zoom keeps drawing while it is full screen and you work elsewhere. Nothing here is transcribed or sent, so it costs nothing, and you keep taking notes the way you do now.

Before classabout 5 minutes

Set up three Terminal windows and Zoom's speaker

In Zoom, open Settings, Audio, and set Speaker to “LectureLive Loopback”. Keep the Mac's own output on your speakers or headphones. If the tool you take notes with stops hearing Zoom after this, set Zoom's speaker back and leave V01 for another lecture.

Commands

Terminal 1: the app's page server (leave it running)

cd "$HOME/Documents/Tools/LectureLive/apps/desktop" && npm run dev

Terminal 2: the app in record-only mode (it waits until you choose a window)

cd "$HOME/Documents/Tools/LectureLive"
D="$HOME/Library/Application Support/LectureLive/m5-recording/Weeks/Week 01 — Recording"
mkdir -p "$D"
LECTURELIVE_CHECK=record LECTURELIVE_CHECK_DIR="$D" \
  LECTURELIVE_RECORD="$HOME/Library/Application Support/LectureLive/m5-fixtures/zoom-live-$(date +%Y%m%d)" \
  ./target/debug/desktop

Terminal 3: kept free for V01 and V03

Record-only mode starts no lecture: it records what the slide detector sees from the window you choose, for 40 minutes, then closes the app. The frames stay on this Mac in m5-fixtures/.

As it startsV01M1 gate

Record 30 minutes of the lecture's sound with no unexplained gaps

Closes M1's gate line “A 30-minute Zoom recording with no unexplained captured-audio gaps”.

Proves the loopback path carries a real Zoom lecture for half an hour without losing any of it. Every automated run used synthetic sound.

Commands and what to expect

In Terminal 3, once the lecturer is speaking

L=$HOME/Documents/Tools/LectureLive/target/debug/lecturelive
$L loopback check --secs 15          # while Zoom plays; pass above −60 dBFS
$L record --loopback --secs 1800 --dir "$HOME/Library/Application Support/LectureLive/m1-zoom"

After class

cat "$HOME/Library/Application Support/LectureLive/m1-zoom/.live_notes/lecture_notes_$(date +%Y%m%d).v2.json"   # "gaps": []

Expected

  • The level check passes above −60 dBFS.
  • The recording stops by itself after 1,800 seconds.
  • The file shows "gaps": [], or only gaps with a stated cause (the receiver unplugged, for instance).

If it fails

A gap with no stated cause: mark Failed, keep the m1-zoom folder and note the time. M6's gate is zero unexplained missing audio, so it starts there.

As it startsV02M5

Watch Zoom's window in the app, with the speaker's camera left out

From M5's list “Needing the person”. The Zoom gate held only with the camera left out, and this is its first use on a live meeting.

In the app from Terminal 2, click Choose window, pick Zoom's meeting window, and drag over the shared slide without Zoom's controls. Then click Leave out a part and drag over the speaker's camera wherever Zoom draws it on the slide. Finish with Watch this region. Recording starts at once and runs for 40 minutes.

What to expect, and after class

Expected

  • The camera shows dimmed, like everything outside the region. The strip reads “Watching”.
  • After class the report lists a decision near each slide change, and none when only the camera moved.
cat "$HOME/Library/Application Support/LectureLive/m5-checks/record.json"

Keep

The folder m5-fixtures/zoom-live-<date>. M6 measures the detector on it, a live meeting rather than a recording's playback. It never leaves this Mac and is deleted once measured.

While a slide is sharedV03M0 and M1 gates

The packaged canary captures Zoom's meeting window, and the image shows the slide

Closes M0's deferred gate line, repeated in M1: “the packaged .app captures the Zoom meeting window with a shared slide, and the image shows the slide”.

Proves a packaged, signed app gets Screen Recording and captures a real meeting window, not only windows the checks opened. It takes a minute, any time a slide is on screen.

Commands and what to expect 
APP="$HOME/Documents/Tools/LectureLive/target/release/bundle/macos/LectureLive Canary.app"
open -W -n "$APP" --args --check windows; tail -n 40 "$HOME/Library/Application Support/LectureLive/canary/checks.log"
open -W -n "$APP" --args --check capture <ID of the zoom.us meeting window>

Expected

  • The listing has a zoom.us “Zoom Meeting” line; its first number is the ID.
  • The capture line in checks.log names canary/window_<ID>.png. Open it: the shared slide is there.

If it fails

“not granted”: allow LectureLive Canary in System Settings, Privacy & Security, Screen & System Audio Recording, then run the capture again.

About 10 minutes inV04M5 open thread

Zoom full screen, with another app in front for five minutes

Answers M5's open thread: whether Zoom keeps drawing while it is full screen on another desktop. Capture itself was shown working there, on a window the app owns.

While V02 records, put Zoom's meeting window in full screen. Then switch to another app or desktop and stay there five minutes or more while the slides change. Come back, and note roughly when you left and when you returned.

What to expect
  • In record.json, “Found again” around the switch, and no “Asking”.
  • Decisions with times inside the minutes Zoom was out of sight.

If no decision arrived while Zoom was out of sight

Zoom stops drawing when hidden. Capture only reads what an app has drawn, and a web page behaved the same way at M5. Mark Needs follow-up: M6 then decides whether Zoom must stay visible, and the app says so.

V05M5, title bar

Move the window by its top strip, and double-click it to zoom

The grey title bar is gone: the status strip is the window's top edge, with the traffic lights at its left. No pointer automation could try it.

What to expect
  • Dragging anywhere in the strip that is not a button moves the window, words included.
  • The strip's buttons (today's spend, Aa, Key) still just click.
  • A double-click zooms the window, and another restores it.
  • The traffic lights sit level with “Ready”.
V06M4, M5

Look at the app in light and dark, and with large type

Every view was checked in a browser engine and in screenshots. This is you reading it at arm's length. Switch macOS between Light and Dark, and press Aa in the strip for large type.

What to expect
  • Everything is readable, and nothing overlaps the traffic lights.
  • The window and region picker (Choose…) shows parts left out as dimmed boxes with a × each.
  • Known and deferred to M6: at the narrowest window (960 px) with large type, the strip's clock is cut off, and the traffic lights sit a little high.
V07M4

Study page opens in your browser

The study page was typeset from the app at M4, but a check never opens the default browser, so the opening itself is yours to see. With a lecture folder that has notes, click Study page once. It typesets for a few minutes (the M4 run took 213 s and cost $0.10), then opens.

What to expect
  • The page opens in your default browser, and its maths is typeset.
V08M4

Answer the Keychain once with Always Allow

After each rebuild of the development app, macOS may ask whether LectureLive can read its key from the Keychain. Choose Always Allow.

What to expect
  • The app starts and the next launch does not ask again, until the next rebuild. The prompt after each rebuild is an M6 open thread.

Decisions only you can make

Open threads that need an answer, not a check. Write the answer in the note, then set the status to Decided.

D1M4 open thread

May Polish run after a lecture has ended?

Today Polish runs only inside a lecture, in the app as in the Python tool. Allowing it afterwards would let you tidy a lecture's notes later, and that is work M6 would add.

D2M4 open thread

Allow a production build, so the built app's security policy is checked

In development the page's content security policy comes from SvelteKit. In a built app it is SvelteKit's tag together with Tauri's header, and no run has checked that pair, because builds wait for your go-ahead. Say yes to let M6 build once and check it.
